I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Neurology at UCSF, working with [Dr. Reza Abbasi-Asl](https://abbasilab.org/), and a Research Data Scientist at the San Francisco VA Health Care System. I develop causal and interpretable machine learning methods for neuroscience and medicine. These methods go beyond prediction and show how a result comes about, for example which neurons drive which, or which clinically relevant cues an AI tool relies on when checking blood pressure technique, such as arm and foot position. They are also validated against independent ground truth, such as synaptic wiring in the brain or clinicians' assessments.

**Computational Neuroscience.** I develop causal inference methods that identify directed interactions in large-scale neural recordings without assuming a predefined model of neural dynamics. Applied to more than 57,000 neurons in [mouse visual cortex](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.31.715474), the connections inferred by my method, CITS, matched the synaptic wiring in the electron-microscopy connectome much better than Granger causality or lagged correlation. Current projects include state-dependent microscale circuitry in visual cortex, directed network dynamics of chronic pain with [Dr. Prasad Shirvalkar](https://www.shirvalkarlab.com/), and AI-based reconstruction of visual experience from fMRI with the [Gallant Lab](https://gallantlab.org/) (UC Berkeley).

**Computational Clinical Diagnostics.** I build AI tools for clinical measurement and diagnosis that hold up outside ideal conditions. With [Dr. Julio Lamprea](https://profiles.ucsf.edu/julio.lampreamontealegre) (UCSF Cardiovascular Prevention Center), I developed [Coach-BP](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.29.26366628), a computer vision system that detects errors in blood pressure measurement technique from clinic video. With [Dr. Maria Wei](https://weilab.ucsf.edu/) (UCSF Dermatology and SF VA), I am developing skin-lesion classifiers designed to stay accurate when photographs contain common artifacts such as hair, ink markings, rulers, blur and uneven lighting.

I completed my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering (with a secondary MS in Statistics) at the University of Washington, advised by [Dr. Eli Shlizerman](https://faculty.washington.edu/shlizee/), with dissertation [*Causal Functional Connectivity from Neural Dynamics*](https://digital.lib.washington.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/4e946c85-b002-418d-bf17-fa1d8549ab71/content), and hold a BS and MS in Statistics from the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.

I also maintain open-source implementations of my methods, including [CITS](https://github.com/abbasilab/cits) and [TimeAwarePC](https://github.com/shlizee/TimeAwarePC).
